Tower Defence Basic Towers, by Nido
https://opengameart.org/content/tower-defence-basic-towers
Released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain). Included turret, cannon, missile launcher, and projectile PNGs are retained as layered source art; projectile sprites are used in combat.

Industrial building sprites are original images generated for this application and processed into transparent individual assets. Original map backgrounds were generated for this application.

Thirty isometric tower variants are original artwork generated for this application and processed into individually addressable transparent sprites.
